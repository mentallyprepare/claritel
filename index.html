What changes in practice

More capacity for the work that needs people.

Available beyond the shift

Routine requests, follow-ups and qualification can continue outside staffed hours, while escalation rules protect the conversations that require a person.

Volume without a larger queue

Run concurrent inbound and outbound conversations within account and campaign controls. Add capacity without adding desks, diallers or layers of supervision.

Better use of human attention

Clara filters intent, gathers context and completes repeatable tasks before handing over. Human agents begin with the customer's history and the reason they need help.

Consistency that can be audited

Use the same knowledge, disclosures, workflows and quality criteria across every interaction. Review exceptions and evidence instead of relying on a small sample.

What teams usually ask before they begin.

How is Clara different from a self-serve voice AI builder?
A builder helps you create an agent. Clara combines the builder with voice, chat, quality analysis and a deployment process tied to the result you want to improve. Your team can build directly, work with ours, or combine both.
Can Clara use our existing telephony?
Yes. Clara can work with compatible SIP infrastructure, or you can use Claritel's cloud telephony. Final architecture depends on your providers, regions and call flows.
What happens when Clara cannot resolve a request?
You set the handoff rules. Clara can escalate when confidence is low, when a customer asks for a person or when a workflow requires human approval. The receiving agent gets the conversation context.
Can we test before making live calls?
Yes. Clara Voice includes in-browser testing so your team can validate prompts, knowledge and tools before using telephony minutes.
Which Indian languages are supported?
Clara supports multilingual voice experiences and mid-conversation language changes. We publish the final language list with native, fluent and conversational proficiency clearly separated. Verify language matrix
How are recordings and transcripts handled?
Your agreement defines ownership, access, portability, retention and deletion. This answer links to the final data-processing and Trust Centre documentation rather than making a blanket claim.
How is Clara priced?
Pricing combines the products and infrastructure you use—for example connected voice time, messaging, model usage, evaluations, plan features and concurrency. Verified INR rates and pass-through fees appear on the Pricing page. Pricing to be confirmed
